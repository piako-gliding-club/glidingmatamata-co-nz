The SZD-50 Puchacz was designed by Adam Meus as the successor to the Bocian, the stork that taught a generation of pilots to fly. It is approved for aerobatics, including inverted flight and rolling manoeuvres, which says a lot about how strongly it is built. Its airbrakes open above and below the wings and work so well that it can descend very steeply.

The club also flies a second Puchacz, ZK-GPC.

The Astir was the first glider Burkhart Grob designed, with its first flight in December 1974. It was a hit, and the Club Astir III is a later development of the same line. A glassfibre club glider with solid all-round performance, it gives pilots an affordable first taste of a glass ship.

It is a good all-rounder for stepping up from the training gliders, and has solid cross-country performance for its class.

The DG-1000 family first flew in July 2000 at Speyer in Germany. The DG-1001Club neo is the version aimed at clubs, with winglets tuned for instruction and forgiving stall behaviour. With a glide ratio of over 40:1 and a low sink rate, a student launched on the winch has a good chance of finding a thermal, which means more time in the air to learn from.

At 18 m span it is fully certified for aerobatics, so it is a trainer that can take you from your first flight through to cross-country.

The Bristell NG 5 was designed by Milan Bristela and is a two-seat, side-by-side light-sport aircraft of mostly aluminium construction. BRM Aero has built hundreds of them, and the first NG 5 was registered in New Zealand in 2013.

This is our tow plane. Every glider launch from aerotow starts behind it, which makes it the busiest aircraft in the fleet on a flying day.

## Proving Grounds tasks

Three club tasks are scored automatically through the Proving Grounds system:

- **Les Riesterer Short Course:** 24.6 km. Start below 3,000 ft, finish above 2,000 ft. The best flight wins the [Les Riesterer Short Course Trophy](/trophies/#les-riesterer-short-course-trophy).
- **Catlin:** 104.2 km. Start below 4,000 ft, finish above 1,000 ft. The fastest handicapped time wins the [Catlin Trophy](/trophies/#catlin-trophy).
- **Valley:** 155.0 km. Start below 4,000 ft, finish above 1,000 ft.

All start, finish and turnpoints are cylinders with a 0.5 km radius. Tasks can be flown clockwise or anticlockwise.

### Submitting your flight

Email your .igc trace to **piako@soaringtasks.com**. It's scored automatically, and a summary of completed tasks is emailed straight back. Declarations and certified loggers aren't required. Any .igc file will do, as long as it has your name and glider type in it.

Proving Grounds handicaps are different to the ones commonly used in NZ contests. For the current list, email piako@soaringtasks.com with the word "handicap" in the subject line. To get the club's Proving Ground .cup file of turnpoints and tasks, email the same address with "task" anywhere in the subject.

### The task boards

The fastest flights are recorded on slips on the task boards in the clubrooms, ordered fastest at the top. Fill in a slip with your name, aircraft and registration, date and handicapped average speed, and move the slips to keep them in order. When a board is full, wipe the slowest slip and reuse it.

If a task fails the email check but is proven good in SeeYou, an Official Observer can sign the slip with a validated time. Feedback on the platform goes to [soaringtasks.com](https://soaringtasks.com).

## Care 200

A 205.9 km ridge task. Thank Steve Care for this one. The best handicapped time wins the [Care 200 Trophy](/trophies/#care-200-trophy).

FAI rules apply: you must enter the FAI sector around each turnpoint, and the difference between start and finish height must be less than 3,280 ft. GPS required. Open to all classes. GNZ handicaps apply.

## Dave McPherson One Diamond Trophy task

An FAI 300 km Diamond Goal task starting and finishing at Matamata Airfield. The fastest handicapped speed wins the [Dave McPherson 1 Diamond Trophy](/trophies/#dave-mcpherson-1-diamond-trophy).

If you're flying the task for your Diamond, make sure you comply with all IGC requirements, including declarations and an Official Observer.

- **Start and finish:** Waharoa Dairy Factory or Matamata Airfield. Start height must be no more than 1,000 m QFE (3,280 ft). Finish high enough for a standard rejoin for the runway in use.
- **Scoring:** the standard GNZ handicap system applies. Speed is total distance flown ÷ elapsed time ÷ handicap. For example, 315 km in 3 hours 40 minutes in a glider with a handicap of 0.94 is 315 ÷ 220 minutes × 60 = 85.9 km/h, ÷ 0.94 = **91.38 km/h** handicapped.
- **Evidence:** get your flight information, including GPS evidence, to the [Chief Flying Instructor](/contact/#chief-flying-instructor) within three weeks of flying the task.

Dave McPherson suggested these tasks when he first set up the trophy (as the Century 21 trophy). Each starts and finishes at Waharoa Dairy Factory:

1. Drury (south boundary of airstrip), Tokoroa (south boundary of airstrip): 302.7 km
2. Drury (south boundary of airstrip), Te Kuiti (south end of pond): 313.3 km
3. Maramarua (school hall), Bennydale (school hall): 302.7 km
4. Rangitaiki (north end of airstrip), Waitoa (SH 26 – Ngarua Rd intersection): 311.7 km
5. Te Puru Bridge (on SH 25), Atiamuri (bridge on SH 1): 309.2 km
6. Hotel de Vin, Atiamuri (bridge on SH 1): 309.0 km

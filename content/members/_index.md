title: Members
heading: Being a member
intro: The club is run entirely by volunteers, so it only works if everyone does their share. Here's what you've signed up for.
rows:
- title: Know how we do things
  text: Every member needs to be familiar with [How We Do Things](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1M2ewDgWizKB7HvEPYSv4Pd2z4RP1ziD3/view?usp=drive_link), our standard operating procedures, and [our Constitution](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1yVw7urcvOSS6Yp1L6OACfQYBzKWmyjnU/view?usp=drive_link), the rules the club runs by.
- title: Duty pilot
  text: 'Unless you''re on the instructor or tow pilot roster, being Duty Pilot is a condition of membership. It comes around once every few months and isn''t onerous. The role is described in section 4.6 of How We Do Things.


    Second-club members aren''t rostered as Duty Pilot. Questions about the role or the roster go to the [Secretary](/contact/#secretary).'
- title: Can't make your rostered day?
  text: 'The roster for the next two weeks is emailed every Wednesday, and is always on the [roster page](https://glidingmatamata.co.nz/members/roster/). Check which days you''re on.


    If you can''t do a day you''re rostered for, **it''s up to you to organise a swap** with another member. Then tell the duty instructor, so they can update the roster.'
- title: Your logbook
  text: A GNZ logbook is mandatory for all glider pilots. You can buy one at the clubrooms — ask the day's duty instructor or the [Chief Flying Instructor](/contact/#chief-flying-instructor). Transfer any temporary logbook entries into it and have an instructor sign them off.
- title: Read the safety newsletter
  text: 'Gliding New Zealand emails a monthly newsletter. Reading it is required, because it carries the flight safety articles. You can [read past editions online](https://gliding.co.nz/gnz-monthly-newsletters).


    Our own club newsletter, The Flypaper, comes out every few months. You can [read past editions of The Flypaper online](http://pgc.wordjot.com/).'
- title: Tow pilots
  text: 'The same rules apply to the tow roster: if you can''t do your day, organise a swap and tell the duty instructor. Our [Tugmaster](/contact/#tugmaster) is your first point of contact for anything towing related.


    Keep up with the [MOAP](https://gliding.co.nz/pilots/moap/) and GNZ''s [Advisory Circulars](https://gliding.co.nz/pilots/moap/advisory-circular/).'
- title: Pitch in
  text: Gliding is a team effort. Expect to help get the aircraft out in the morning, launch and retrieve gliders during the day, and put everything back in the hangar when flying finishes.
- title: Useful links
  text: '- [MOAP](https://gliding.co.nz/pilots/moap/) — Gliding New Zealand''s Manual of Approved Procedures

    - [Pilot Training Program](https://training.gliding.co.nz/)

    - [gliding.net.nz](https://gliding.net.nz) — the national membership database, events and glider tracking. Register with the email address from your membership application.'

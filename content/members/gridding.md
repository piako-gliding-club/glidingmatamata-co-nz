## Bookings

- Please make a booking, so it's clear if it's going to be busy, and we can contact you.
- Booking times are **not** launch times. They're just an indication of when you're aiming to be ready to go.
- If the bookings are busy, don't plan to do checkrides or circuits during the main launch rush, usually from around 11:30. Do them earlier or later in the day.

## On runway 28

- Don't drive a glider down in front of the tow plane about to launch. Wait up by the hangars. The best time to drive down is immediately after a launch.
- If you're halfway down the runway and the tow plane is about to launch, keep going to get out of the way. Don't try to stop halfway down or squeeze over by the fence. (Tow pilots: don't tow with anyone halfway down the runway.)
- Don't try to squeeze between gridded gliders and the caravan. Drive around the grid after checking for landing traffic.
- Tip: get as ready as you can before towing down, so you can grid as soon as you arrive and be ready to go.
- When we're winching on 28, park your car down towards the end of the runway.

## On runway 10

- Make sure the caravan is parked beside the clubhouse fence.
- Don't block the ramp up to the Matamata Aeroclub.
- Leave room for taxiing aircraft.

## Gridding

- The grid sets the launch order.
- Only grid when you're actually ready to launch. Otherwise leave the glider off the side of the runway.
- The training twin may push in occasionally, as it can't queue in the grid while it's in the air. Instructors will avoid circuit training and high tows during busy periods.
- If you're not ready when the tow plane arrives, you'll be pushed off to the side and go to the back of the grid.
- If it's really busy, avoid high tows to help speed up the launch for those behind you.

## Housekeeping

- You can pay for your tow before your flight — handy if you're coming back late.
- Otherwise, remember to pay for your tow before you leave at the end of the day.

Have a great time out there!

---
title: Flying on non-rostered club days
parent: /members/documents
intro: Club members may fly on mid-week days that aren't regular scheduled flying days, as long as the conditions below are met.
---
These days are usually arranged to take advantage of cross-country soaring conditions, and pilots are expected to fly away from the local area on planned tasks. So it's important that a nominated person is responsible for all flying operations, and knows who is flying and what their task intentions are.

## Before the day

- Plan the day with prior approval from the [Chief Flying Instructor](/contact/#chief-flying-instructor) or their appointed deputy, before flying starts.

## On the day

- Every pilot flying must tell the Chief Flying Instructor or appointed deputy their intentions, and when they've returned.
- A group of pilots may nominate a Coordinator to tell the Chief Flying Instructor everyone's intentions, and confirm all gliders are back at the end of the day.
- Keep a flying time sheet recording the day's activity. Each pilot notes their cross-country flight intentions on the time sheet before they take off.
- Only QGP pilots may fly cross-country on these days.

## Training

- Independent Operations days may be approved for flying training in singles or twins, only under direct supervision by a B Cat instructor.

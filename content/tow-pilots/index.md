  - title: What it involves
    text: Flying our tow plane to launch gliders to release height, then returning for the next tow. A day's flying is short, repeated hops rather than long cross-country legs.
  - title: What you'll need
    text: An NZ PPL (or working towards one), and a tow endorsement from one of our instructors — training is provided at the club.
  - title: What you get
    text: Flying hours, currency, and a discounted or free membership in exchange for towing duty — details depend on how often you're rostered on.
  - title: The club
    text: We're a volunteer-run club — tow pilots work alongside duty pilots, instructors, and ground crew to keep flying running each day we're out.

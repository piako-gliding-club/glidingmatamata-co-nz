options:
  - title: Flying with our instructors
    tag: The simple option
    text: No medical, club membership, or NZ glider pilot licence required. Book a trial flight or twin + instructor slot on our website, same as any visitor. We generally charge club rates for one or two day visits.
  - title: Flying solo in our club gliders
    tag: For qualified pilots
    text: Join our club (this also joins Gliding New Zealand), bring proof of your overseas membership and logbook, convert your licence with an instructor (essentially a Biennial Flight Review), get a local airfield briefing, and hold a valid medical.
briefing_intro: Matamata is a busy airfield with light aircraft, flight training, parachuting, gyrocopters and model aircraft. Most visiting traffic has no understanding of glider operations, so please read these notes before you fly. Visiting glider pilots who want to fly club gliders have additional requirements.
briefing:
  - title: Ground operations
    text: Vehicles on the active runway use flashing lights and stay only as long as needed. Park on the down-wind side of the launch point, at the edge of the runway and one car deep. Stop for any aircraft taking off or landing.
  - title: Circuits
    text: Circuits for runway 10/28 are on the south-western side, and for 04/22 on the north-western side. Gliders and powered aircraft share the circuit. Announce your intentions early, and avoid flying directly overhead the airfield.
  - title: Radio
    text: "Matamata has a mandatory broadcast zone: 3 nm radius, surface to 4,500 ft, on 122.25 MHz. Gliders use 133.55 MHz outside it."
  - title: Skydiving
    text: Parachute landing areas are Kaimai (runway 04/22) and Clubroom. Listen for the drop pilot's broadcast, and keep out of the drop sector after the 3-minute call.
  - title: Model aircraft
    text: Models fly from the inactive runway, up to 1,100 ft AMSL. Crosses at the threshold show when they are operating.
  - title: Airspace
    text: Controlled airspace starts at 4,500 ft, and can be raised to 6,500 ft locally by asking ATC to open G254 Matamata (Bay Approach, 119.5 MHz).

good_to_know:
  - Staying longer term? Members are expected to help out — duty pilot, instructor, or tow pilot.
  - Gliders aren't available for full-day use without prior arrangement. Twin flights are usually 1–2 hours.
  - Basic accommodation is available on-site via the [Matamata Soaring Centre ↗](https://msc.gliding.co.nz/accommodation) — plan to arrive on a flying day, or organise a key in advance.
  - Public transport to the airfield is limited — we recommend hiring a car.
